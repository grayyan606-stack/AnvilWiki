## What should you check before buying a Steal an Egg gamepass?

1. Open the pass from the [official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/107778070777162/Steal-An-Egg), not from a shared link.
2. Read the exact effect, duration, stack limit, and exclusions on the official pass card.
3. Record your current income or speed before purchase.
4. Test the effect in your own base and note the after value.
5. Compare the observed benefit with your goal using the [income calculator](/tools/income-calculator) or [speed calculator](/tools/speed-calculator).

<Callout type="warn" title="Prices and effects change">Do not rely on screenshots or resale offers. Check the live official pass card every time; prices, bonuses, and availability can change without notice.</Callout>

## Why does this page not list every gamepass?

Because the game updates quickly and purchase details are account- and platform-sensitive. Publishing a copied list without a live check can mislead players. This wiki will add a verified pass only when the name, official effect, price, and check date can be confirmed from the official experience page.

## How do you decide if a pass is worth it?

Ask three questions:

- Does it solve a real bottleneck in your current loop?
- Can you measure the benefit in your own base?
- Would the same Robux be better spent after the next update?

If any answer is unclear, wait and use the free progression loop first. Start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide) or [how to play](/wiki/how-to-play).

## Is the item a pass or a repeatable product?

Roblox distinguishes passes, which charge a one-time fee for privileges, from developer products that can be purchased repeatedly. A store button should therefore be evaluated by its actual product description, not by a player casually calling every paid item a “gamepass.” See the official [passes documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/passes) and [developer products documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products).

For your own record, write down what the item says it grants and whether the purchase is repeatable. If a benefit is described as temporary, identify the stated duration and activation condition. If those details are absent, leave them unresolved; do not assume that a familiar icon implies a permanent bonus.

This page does not maintain a verified catalog of this game's current paid items. Its purpose is to help you evaluate the official item you are already considering. No example below is a claim that a particular income pass, speed pass, or timed boost currently exists in the experience.

## What should your pre-purchase note contain?

Make the decision inspectable before spending. A short note is enough:

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Official item link and name | Prevents confusion with another game's product |
| Price shown to your account | Keeps the decision tied to the actual offer you see |
| Exact stated effect | Separates a promised function from a guessed benefit |
| Duration and conditions | Identifies when the benefit applies |
| Current bottleneck | Explains what problem you expect it to solve |
| Current measured baseline | Gives you a comparison if you later purchase |
| Spending limit | Sets the maximum you chose before seeing the prompt |

If you are using a family-managed account or someone else's payment method, follow the account owner's permission and spending arrangements. Do not treat a guide's enthusiasm as permission to purchase. A progression calculator can estimate time under assumptions, but it cannot decide an appropriate personal budget.

## How do you estimate the practical benefit without buying first?

Translate the official description into a conditional question. For a hypothetical item that claims to improve a measured rate, ask how much time your next goal would save if that rate changed as described. Keep that estimate separate from an observed result. Marketing language is a proposed input until you have evidence that it applies to your exact setup.

Suppose, purely as an arithmetic example, you need 6,000 more speed and currently measure 10 speed per second. The estimated training time is 600 seconds. If a hypothetical benefit changed the effective rate to 15 per second, the estimate would be 400 seconds, saving 200 seconds for that one goal. These numbers are invented examples, not pass effects or game rates.

The benefit may matter less if training is not what currently slows you down. A faster training rate does not by itself solve a route you cannot navigate, a crowded nest, or a full base without a useful replacement. Identify the actual bottleneck before comparing a paid offer with your current routine.

## How can you check an effect after an independently chosen purchase?

If you decide to buy through Roblox, retain the official item details and your private transaction record. Then compare the relevant measurement under the same conditions used for the baseline. Keep the pet setup, training method, visible event state, and measurement duration consistent wherever they could affect the result.

Change one variable at a time. Buying an item, replacing several pets, and changing training equipment before the next measurement makes it hard to attribute any improvement. Record what changed and avoid claiming that the paid item caused the whole difference.

Measure the unit the description actually affects. A bonus described for one activity should not automatically be applied to every income source in the calculator. If a displayed total already includes the benefit, multiplying that total again would exaggerate it. Repeat a short measurement if the first sample is interrupted, while preserving the interruption in your notes.

## What if the item appears not to work as described?

Check the exact item, account, stated conditions, and any activation instructions in the official description. Preserve the before and after evidence instead of buying the product again as a test. A second purchase is not a reliable troubleshooting method, especially when you are uncertain whether the item is repeatable.

Use the developer's official support route for an experience-specific effect and Roblox's support route for platform transaction or account issues. This wiki cannot view your transaction history, activate a pass, issue a refund, or guarantee a support outcome. Share payment and account records only through the appropriate official process.

For a correction to this guide, send the public item link and the wording that needs clarification through [Contact](/contact). Exclude payment details and authentication information. The useful public lesson is the distinction between the stated effect, the conditions measured, and what remains unresolved.
