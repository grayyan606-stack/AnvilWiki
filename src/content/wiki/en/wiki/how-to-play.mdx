## What is the main objective?

Your immediate objective is to bring eggs back to your base. Each successful return creates another chance to hatch a useful pet. Pets generate money, money funds upgrades, and speed gives you access to more difficult areas and safer returns.

## The six-step loop

1. **Choose an egg route.** Start where you can reliably escape rather than chasing the rarest visible egg.
2. **Steal one egg.** Watch the guardian and nearby players before committing.
3. **Return to base.** Carrying a larger or more contested egg can make the route harder.
4. **Hatch the egg.** Check the resulting pet's base income, size, and mutation.
5. **Upgrade income and capacity.** Replace weak slots only when the new pet actually improves the total.
6. **Train speed.** Measure your gain rate and work toward the next biome with a safety margin.

<Callout type="tip" title="Progression rule">A safe mid-value return is usually better than repeatedly losing the highest-value egg you can barely reach.</Callout>
