## What is the safest way to verify a Steal an Egg badge?

Use this checklist:

1. Open the [official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/107778070777162/Steal-An-Egg) and inspect the badge through Roblox's own interface.
2. Record the exact badge name, unlock condition, reward, and the date you checked it.
3. Complete the condition in your own session before marking it as verified.
4. Separate current badges from removed, renamed, or event badges.
5. Avoid lists that omit a check date or reuse another site's text.

<Callout type="info" title="Unverified means unpublished">This page intentionally avoids guessing badge IDs or unlock strings. A badge should only be added when its condition is repeatable and its source is official.</Callout>

## Why do badge pages become inaccurate quickly?

Badges can be renamed, retired, tied to limited events, or changed by updates. A page that lists every historical badge without dates can confuse new players. The safest structure is to show current unlocks separately and archive removed badges with their last check date.

## How do you use badges without slowing progress?

Badges are useful milestones, but they should not replace your income and speed plan. If an unlock requires a risky steal, wait until you have a safety margin. Then follow the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide) or check the [all biomes reference](/biomes/all-biomes) before entering harder areas.

## How do you distinguish a badge from an in-game milestone?

Roblox describes experience badges as awards that creators can use for accomplishments within their experiences. That general feature does not prove that every milestone mentioned in a video is a Roblox badge. A collection entry, event task, on-screen celebration, and platform badge can refer to different forms of progress. See [Roblox's explanation of experience badges](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313650-How-Do-I-Make-Experience-Badges) for the platform concept.

Begin by finding the actual award through the correct experience on Roblox. Compare the experience identity, badge name, and description. If you can find only a creator's spoken claim and no corresponding official item, record it as an unconfirmed milestone. Do not add a badge ID or unlock requirement from a similarly named game to make the record look complete.

This distinction also prevents a false reward expectation. An award may recognize an accomplishment without the description promising currency, a pet, or an income boost. Record a separate gameplay reward only when it is explicitly stated or directly observed. The existence of a badge alone does not establish that extra benefit.

## What belongs in a useful personal badge record?

Create one record per award rather than a single undated checklist. The following fields separate the identity of the award from your progress toward it:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Official badge URL or ID | Identifies the exact award when names are similar |
| Associated experience | Confirms that the award belongs to the game covered here |
| Name and description | Preserves the wording you actually inspected |
| Check date | Shows when the listing was visible |
| Stated condition | Records the official requirement without embellishment |
| Your attempt | Describes the action you took and when |
| Ownership result | Separates an attempted unlock from a confirmed award |
| Extra reward evidence | Records a gameplay benefit only if separately supported |

Leave unknown fields marked unknown. If the description is vague, do not convert it into a precise count based on a guess. Add the interpretation as a question to investigate. This keeps a personal tracker useful when you return after an update and no longer remember which parts were certain.

## How can you test an unlock condition without confusing the result?

First check whether the account already owns the award. An account that already has it may be unsuitable for testing whether a repeated action triggers a new award notification. Record the starting state before attempting the condition.

Next isolate the relevant action. If you complete several milestones at once and receive an award, you cannot confidently identify which milestone caused it. Where practical, perform one candidate action, inspect the result, and record the sequence. Do not spend Robux or create extra accounts solely to fill gaps in this page.

After the attempt, inspect the account's award state through Roblox. A remembered pop-up and a confirmed ownership record are different evidence. If the expected award is not visible, retain the attempt as inconclusive while checking the exact requirement and experience. One unsuccessful attempt does not prove that a badge has been removed or that the game's implementation is broken.

## What should you do when an expected badge does not appear?

Work through the smallest explanations first. Confirm that you played the correct experience, used the intended account, and satisfied the complete wording of the condition. Check whether the condition described in the guide was official or inferred by another player. Look for a distinction between starting an activity and completing it.

Save a short sequence of what happened, including any relevant on-screen message. Avoid publicly posting a full account screen when a small crop shows the issue. If further help is needed, use a support channel linked by the developer or Roblox itself. This wiki can correct an explanation but cannot award a badge or inspect private account state.

Do not repeatedly attempt a costly action just because an old guide says the reward should appear. An unresolved result is a reason to seek better evidence. Record the time already spent and return to a reliable progression activity while the requirement remains unclear.

## How should historical and event awards be tracked?

Keep “owned,” “currently listed,” and “currently obtainable” as separate statuses. They answer different questions. A player may own a historical award even when a new player cannot reproduce the original conditions. Conversely, failing to find a badge in one browsing session is not enough evidence to declare it permanently retired.

For an event-related claim, preserve the source date and the event context. When availability cannot be confirmed, write “current availability unverified” instead of inventing an end date. A historical note should help a collector understand an old record without inviting new players to spend hours attempting an unavailable challenge.
