## How do you measure speed gain?

1. Record your current speed.
2. Train continuously for at least 30 seconds.
3. Record the new speed.
4. Divide the increase by the sample duration.
5. Repeat once and use the lower or median rate.

## What is the time formula?

`time remaining = (target speed − current speed) ÷ measured gain per second`

Enter the values in the [Speed Goal Calculator](/tools/calculators#speed-calculator).
