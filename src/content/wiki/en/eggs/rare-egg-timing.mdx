## How do you measure a useful cycle?

1. Start a timer when the relevant eggs reset or spawn.
2. Record at least three cycles in the same server.
3. Time a complete base-to-nest-to-base route.
4. Add time for guardian avoidance, other players, and carrying the egg.
5. Use the median instead of the fastest run.

<Callout type="warn" title="Server behavior can differ">A reported five-minute cadence is a useful starting assumption, but events, updates, or server timing can make your observed interval different.</Callout>

## What does the planner calculate?

The [Egg Run Planner](/tools/calculators#run-calculator) compares:

`spawn interval − round trip − escape buffer`

A positive result is spare time. A negative result means the planned route extends beyond the cycle and should be shortened, delayed, or attempted from a closer position.
