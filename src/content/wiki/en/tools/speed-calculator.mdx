## How long does Steal an Egg speed training take?

Use the basic formula:

`total time = (target speed − current speed) ÷ measured gain per second`

Sample your treadmill gain for at least 30 seconds, then repeat once. Use the lower or median gain rate for planning.

## How do you build a realistic daily plan?

1. Record your current speed.
2. Choose the next biome target or a personal safety target.
3. Measure gain for a fixed interval.
4. Enter the number of minutes you can actually train each day.
5. Compare the estimate with your route goals.

<Callout type="info" title="Add a safety margin">A biome entry minimum is not always a comfortable carrying speed. Use the [Biome Readiness Checker](/tools/calculators#biome-calculator) before you carry a larger egg through a harder area.</Callout>

## Why not publish one fixed speed table?

The game updates quickly, and community tables can disagree. This page keeps the calculation honest by using your live inputs. For threshold context and known conflicts, read [all biomes](/biomes/all-biomes) and the [speed training guide](/guides/speed-training).
